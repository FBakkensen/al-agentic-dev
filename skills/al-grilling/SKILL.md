---
name: al-grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when an answer shifts each time it is restated, a preference has no reason under it, or the user asks to stress-test their thinking.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

Ask one question per message, with lettered options and your recommendation marked, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Ask in the reply itself, as plain text — a question tool strips the options and the recommendation.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
